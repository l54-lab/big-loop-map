Lessons from the road

The East Coast is flat

Out west every flight plan has to account for terrain and engine-out options. One day they flew five hours straight and level at 2,000' under a 3,000' overcast — no terrain to worry about at all.

The East Coast has weather

Calibrating what NEXRAD colors actually mean took practice: green was fine, yellow turned out to be manageable (if bug-cleaning) rain, and orange was never tested.

Watch the time zones

The same mistake, three separate times — promising family a 6pm arrival that was really 7pm once the next time zone caught up with them.

Pack light, pack smart

One rollaboard each plus a 10-gallon Home Depot storage bin ("dresser drawers") that stayed in the plane — clothes got swapped between the two during gas stops.

Fuel prices swing hard

ForeFlight's Fuel Price layer let them route lunch stops around cheap gas — averaging $6.25/gallon for the trip, while home base at KPAO was charging $8.40.

Small airports, big kindness

Eight courtesy cars borrowed along the way, several left with keys under the mat after hours — general aviation people are, as the article puts it, "just plain nice."
